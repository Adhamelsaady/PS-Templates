FFT / NTT quick notes for the template book

- FFT: fast polynomial convolution using complex roots of unity; usually O(N log N).
- NTT: the same convolution idea performed modulo an NTT-friendly prime, avoiding floating-point precision errors.
- FWHT: transform for XOR / AND / OR convolution, not ordinary sum-index convolution.
- Convolution: c[k] = sum a[i] * b[k-i]. This is the key reason FFT/NTT appears in pair/triple counting problems.
- Before using a transform, make the transform size a power of two large enough for the required polynomial degree.
- Always check modulus/root requirements for NTT.
